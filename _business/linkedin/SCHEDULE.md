# LinkedIn Weekly Content System

Three posts a week, every week, for 36 weeks:

| Day | Time (Brisbane) | Item | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tuesday** | 7:30am | Article teaser + card image | `posts-a.md` / `posts-b.md` + `images/NN.png` |
| **Thursday** | 12:30pm | Poll (matches the week's article theme) | `polls.md` |
| **Saturday** | 9:00am | Short take on a current AI + hospitality happening | `news-posts.md` (workflow + templates) |

Why these slots: Tuesday morning catches operators before prep; Thursday lunch is LinkedIn's
highest poll-response window; Saturday morning is when owners actually read industry news.

## Scheduling mechanics (the honest version)

- **Teasers**: schedule up to a month ahead with LinkedIn's native scheduler (clock icon next
  to Post). ~15 minutes a month.
- **Polls**: LinkedIn does NOT allow scheduling polls. They must be posted live each Thursday.
  60 seconds: copy the week's poll from `polls.md`, post.
- **News posts**: can't be pre-written, that's the point of them. The Saturday post takes 10
  minutes with the workflow in `news-posts.md`.
- A weekly automation assembles your pack every Monday morning (teaser text, poll, fresh news
  candidates) so the whole week is one copy-paste session.

## Rules

- Article link always goes in the FIRST COMMENT, never the post body.
- If any post gets real traction (20+ reactions, DMs), pause the calendar and follow the thread
  with a same-topic post within 48 hours. Momentum beats the schedule.
- Reply to every single comment. The algorithm rewards it and the DMs are where audit enquiries
  start.
