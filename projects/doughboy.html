Doughboy is pizza production intelligence for multi-venue kitchens. It centralises batch tracking, standardises recipes across locations, and gives kitchen leads real-time visibility into stock, compliance, and team performance — without the spreadsheets, without the group chats, without the end-of-service surprises.

Built by someone who's spent two decades inside hospitality. Every feature exists because a real kitchen needed it.

// Production intelligence for every venue, every shift.

The Problem

Pizza kitchens running across multiple venues face four problems that never go away: nobody knows the actual dough ball count until service is already broken, temperature variations wreck consistency batch to batch, new staff make errors that reach the customer before anyone catches them, and the head chef's standards don't automatically travel to every location on every shift.

Most operators patch these problems with WhatsApp groups, shared spreadsheets, and institutional knowledge that leaves when people do. Doughboy replaces that stack with a single system built specifically for the job.

What It Does

Live Dashboard

Total dough balls across all venues, batch compliance status, end-of-day reporting, and real-time alerts for missing reports or training gaps — visible to the kitchen lead at a glance. No chasing, no guessing.

Multi-Dough Configuration

Configure unlimited dough types — NY Style, Detroit, Focaccia, Neapolitan, sourdough, anything. Custom hydration percentages, fermentation times, and method notes per type. Production staff select a dough type and the parameters auto-populate. No memorisation. No interpretation.

Weather-Adjusted Recommendations

Live forecast data integrates before production begins. On hot days, Doughboy flags hydration adjustments, ice usage, and autolyse modifications automatically. Consistency across seasons, not just across recipes.

Batch Planning & History

Weekly production calendars with target vs. actual tracking. Every batch logged with dough type, temperatures, hydration, autolyse details, and quality scores. Pattern detection over time — not just records.

Cross-Venue Stock Tracking

Each venue submits daily counts. The kitchen lead sees consolidated inventory across all locations in one view. Stock levels, not silence.

Training Management

Eight structured training modules, fully controlled by the head chef — content, videos, completion tracking per venue. Standards don't drift when the chef isn't in the room.

Staff Broadcasts

Push urgent messages — recipe changes, hot-day adjustments, process updates — to all venues or specific locations. No group chats. No missed messages.

Role-Based Access

Configurable permissions across Head Chef, Dough Production, Pizza Chef, and Kitchen Staff roles. Everyone sees what they need. Nothing they don't.

The Build

Doughboy runs entirely in the browser — no software installation, no downloads, setup under ten minutes. The backend runs on Supabase for authentication, real-time data sync, and multi-venue data isolation. Payments are handled through Stripe. It deploys as a static site on Vercel.

Built in Brisbane. Designed around how kitchens actually operate, not how software vendors imagine they do.
