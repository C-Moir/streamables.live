# Getting Paid - invoice template + payment setup

## One-time setup (do this before the first audit is sold, ~15 minutes)

**Stripe Payment Link** - you already run Stripe for DoughBoy, so this is trivial:
1. Stripe Dashboard → Payment Links → New
2. Product: "Venue Operations & AI Audit" · Price: $1,650 AUD · GST: inclusive
   (confirm your Stripe tax settings handle GST the way your accountant wants)
3. Create a second link: "Audit - additional site" · $550 AUD
4. Save both URLs at the bottom of this file. From then on, "send me the link" is the
   entire payment conversation.

Card fees (~1.7% + 30c ≈ $29 on $1,650) are the cost of same-day payment. Put your bank
details on the invoice too and let the client pick; bank transfer saves the fee, Stripe
gets you paid faster. Both beat chasing.

## Sequencing (per the engagement letter)

1. Client replies "Agreed" → visit booked
2. Visit happens → report delivered within 5 business days
3. **Invoice goes out WITH the report** - the report email and the invoice are the same email
4. Day 7 unpaid → one-line nudge: "Bumping the invoice from last week - link's in the
   thread if card is easier."
5. Day 14 unpaid → phone call, not email. At this price and proximity, a call resolves it.

## Bookkeeping minimum

A spreadsheet with: invoice #, date, client, amount ex-GST, GST, paid date. Your accountant
needs the GST column at BAS time. Number invoices sequentially from 2026-001 and never
reuse a number.
