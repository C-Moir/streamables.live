# Weekly Polls (Thursdays, 12:30pm Brisbane)

One per week, themed to that week's article. LinkedIn limits: question ≤140 characters,
2-4 options, ≤30 characters each. Run each poll for 1 week. **Polls cannot be scheduled on
LinkedIn - post them live.**

After the poll closes, comment on it with your take + a link to the matching article. That
second touch is where the poll pays off.
