CLAWLOSSEUM

Live — v1.0.0
→ Enter the Arena
//

CLAWLOSSEUM is live. You build an AI agent — your gladiator. You enter the arena. You answer real coding and cybersecurity questions under time pressure, and your fighter takes damage or deals it based on whether you get it right, how fast you answer, and the stats you've built over time.

It runs entirely in the browser. No downloads. No installs. Free to start.

// The arena. Gladiators have entered.

How It Works

You create a fighter from one of six classes — Hacker, Code Wizard, Pentester, Defender, Cryptographer, or Social Engineer. Each class has a different stat distribution and unique perks that unlock as you level up. You allocate points across Strength, Agility, Intelligence, and Defence, then enter the arena.

Battles are five rounds. Each round presents a real question. You see the question, pick your answer, and race a countdown timer. Your fighter's stats affect accuracy, response speed, and damage dealt. Answer correctly and you hit hard. Get it wrong and you take damage. Land a critical hit by answering correctly AND faster than your opponent.

This isn't a spectator game. You're the one fighting.

The Three Arenas

Cyber Gauntlet

Network security, encryption, malware types, CVEs, penetration testing, SSL/TLS, DNS, cloud security. 50 questions across three difficulty tiers. The flagship arena for anyone in security.

Code Colosseum

Algorithms, data structures, Big-O complexity, JavaScript, Python, debugging, design patterns, recursion, async programming. 50 questions. Speed and correctness win.

Opsec Gauntlet

Social engineering, phishing, incident response, access control, GDPR, threat modelling, physical security, insider threats. 50 questions. Think like a defender.

Progression

Fighters level from 1 to 50 across five gladiator ranks: Tiro (Novice), Auctorati (Pledged Fighter), Primus (First Among Many), Doctore (Arena Master), and Imperator (Emperor of Blades). Each rank spans ten levels with escalating XP requirements.

Every class unlocks 10 perks as you level — abilities like Zero Latency (bonus damage on the first correct answer), Honeypot (chance for opponent damage to backfire), Packet Storm (faster response time on network questions), or Ghost Machine (ignore a percentage of opponent defence). Perks change how you fight, not just how strong you are.

Battle Scars are achievements that unlock permanent stat bonuses. Win your first fight. Survive with under 10 HP. Hit a 5-win streak. Stack 50 kills. Each scar marks your fighter's history and makes them mechanically stronger.

The Codex

Every fighter has a codex — a markdown document where you write their battle philosophy, strengths, weaknesses, battle cry, and lore. This isn't cosmetic. The word count of your codex directly boosts your fighter's accuracy in battle, up to +8%. A well-written fighter literally fights smarter.

A randomiser generates unique codex profiles from over 21 million possible combinations for players who want a quick start. But the best fighters have hand-written codexes. The arena rewards those who put in the work.

Tokens & Ranked Combat

CLAWLOSSEUM has a token economy built in. You earn tokens through arena combat and use them to unlock ranked matchmaking and premium features. The ranked ladder separates committed fighters from casual challengers — to enter ranked combat you put tokens on the line. Win and you climb. Lose and you pay the price.

Payments are handled through NOWPayments with the API key secured server-side via Vercel serverless functions. The economy is designed to be fair at free tier and meaningfully competitive at ranked.

Live Online PvP

Players queue into real-time matchmaking and fight other humans anywhere in the world. Both players see the same questions in the same arena. Answers sync live — you answer, your opponent answers, and the round resolves in real time. If your opponent disconnects, you win by forfeit.

There's also a Bot vs Bot spectator mode where you watch two legendary AI fighters tear each other apart. No skill required. Pure spectacle.

The Build

CLAWLOSSEUM is built entirely in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No framework. No build step. No dependencies. Sound effects are procedurally generated through the Web Audio API — there are zero audio files in the entire project. The retro 8-bit aesthetic uses CSS scanline overlays, pixel fonts, and hand-crafted ASCII fighter art.

The backend runs on Supabase for authentication, leaderboard syncing, cloud saves, and real-time matchmaking. The entire application deploys as a static site with two API routes. It went from concept to live product in a single build session.
